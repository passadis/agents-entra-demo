01 / THE BUSINESS PROBLEM

A ship leaves tonight.
A depot needs supplies.

Piraeus needs safety seals and an engine sensor. Newark needs replenishment too. Agents can propose orders. The organization must control who can act, where, for how much, and under whose oversight.

03 / AUTHENTICATION

The host proves trust.
The agent carries authority.

Managed identity → blueprint exchange token → agent resource token.

The blueprint request carries fmi_path=<agent-id>. The tool verifies the resource token's signature, issuer, audience, lifetime, identity, and role.

A workforce approval is a business decision. The agent still executes under its own identity.

04 / LIVE WALKTHROUGH

Five requests.

05 / GOVERNANCE LIFECYCLE

Authority must have
a lifecycle.

Suspend one agent. Pause its class. Expire its review. Re-evaluate a pending approval after policy changes.

Local controls in the lab enforce application policy. Tenant Conditional Access, identity risk controls, and native governance are configured separately.

07 / KNOW WHAT THE DEMO PROVES

Identity is necessary.
Policy completes the decision.

Simulation proves local enforcement. Live mode adds real Entra token acquisition and validation. Purchase orders remain fictional in both.

Production needs transactional storage, a separate tool service, protected audit export, and tenant governance. The JSON ledger is a single-process presentation store.
