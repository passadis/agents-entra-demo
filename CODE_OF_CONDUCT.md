# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a welcoming, respectful place to learn and contribute. Everyone should be able to participate without harassment, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, experience, or background.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate. Discuss ideas and implementation choices without attacking people.
- Give constructive feedback, accept correction, and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience. Make room for other contributors.
- Respect privacy, consent, and other people's time.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or sustained unwanted contact.
- Sexualized language or imagery, sexual attention, or advances in project spaces.
- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, or deliberate disruption of discussion.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern or participates in a review.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone represents the project in public. Respectful technical disagreement is welcome.

## Reporting concerns

The repository owner, [Konstantinos Passadis (@passadis)](https://github.com/passadis), is responsible for handling concerns. Use a private contact method published on the owner's profile or linked website. If none is available, open an issue asking only for a private contact channel, without describing the incident or naming affected people. Do not post private details publicly or use vulnerability reporting for conduct concerns.

You may also use GitHub's reporting and blocking tools for abuse on the platform. If a concern involves the owner, report platform abuse directly to [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports fairly, handle personal information as privately as possible, and consider the impact and context of the behavior. They may request clarification before taking action. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed when platform escalation is needed.

Responses may include a request to stop, a warning, removal of content, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban from project spaces. Serious or repeated abuse may lead directly to a ban. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy and will communicate the reason where practical.

Participants must respect enforcement decisions and avoid retaliation. Requests for reconsideration should use the same private reporting channel and provide relevant new information.
